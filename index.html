Privacy Policy

Free Video Tools · last updated August 3, 2026

1. Summary

Free Video Tools (“the app”) is a video editing and conversion toolbox published by Cool App Helpdesk. It trims, compresses, converts, merges, and applies filters and effects to videos, and extracts audio.

  • All videos and audio files are processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • The app contains non-intrusive advertising via Google AdMob.
  • The app does not collect personal data beyond the advertising ID collected by AdMob.

2. Data we collect

The app itself does not collect personal data. However, the Google AdMob SDK integrated in the app may collect certain information:

3. Data stored on your device

All videos, audio files, and exported results remain exclusively on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly share a file through Android’s system share sheet.

4. Permissions used

The app requests no storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. Videos are opened and saved through Android’s Storage Access Framework, which grants access to a specific file without broad permissions.

PermissionReasonWhen asked

5. Network activity and third parties

Video processing and all 16 tools work fully offline. The app integrates Google AdMob for non-intrusive advertising. AdMob may collect device information, advertising IDs, and IP addresses to serve ads. This data is managed by Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or attribution SDKs. All other libraries operate entirely on-device and do not transmit data.

When you share a video via the system share sheet, the app passes a local file URI to the app you choose. The developer does not see what you share.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data you create in Free Video Tools remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Free Video Tools → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Exported videos can be deleted manually through your device’s file manager.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
